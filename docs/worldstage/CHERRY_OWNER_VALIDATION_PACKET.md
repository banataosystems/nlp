# Cherry owner workflow validation packet

**Project:** WorldStage / Cherry Adaptive Operating System  
**Purpose:** validate how WorldStage actually works before adding secure intake, CRM, calendar, participant, payment, or AI production integrations.

This is not a software requirements interview. It is a short operating-truth conversation. The product should adapt to the answers.

## How to use this packet

Ask these in one focused conversation. Use Cherry's exact language. Do not correct terminology during the interview. Capture examples, exceptions, and what she dislikes about current tools.

## The 15 questions

1. **When a good new client first appears, what usually happens from that first message or introduction until they actually work with WorldStage?**

2. **What do you personally need to understand about a client before you are comfortable recommending any program?**

3. **What is the difference, in your own words, between a discovery conversation, an FGD, and a “twinkle” conversation?**

4. **What information usually changes the direction of an intervention after the first brief?**

5. **Which decisions must come to you personally, and which should your team decide without interrupting you?**

6. **When you open your phone in the morning, what are the five things you most need to know about the business?**

7. **What promises or follow-ups are most dangerous for the team to forget?**

8. **Before you walk into a client meeting, keynote, workshop, or difficult conversation, what would the perfect 60-second briefing tell you?**

9. **After a program, how do you personally decide whether the intervention really worked? What evidence do you trust?**

10. **What should never be shown to a client sponsor even if WorldStage collected it from participants or facilitators?**

11. **What information should never be put into AI, analytics, screenshots, public repositories, or a general company knowledge system?**

12. **Where does the business currently keep the truth for contacts, proposals, schedules, client history, program materials, participant information, payments, and follow-ups?**

13. **Which current tools do you want to keep because the team already works well with them, and which ones create the most friction?**

14. **If we make only one real workflow live first, which would save you the most time or prevent the most mistakes?**

15. **What would make you reject this system even if the technology works perfectly?**

## Evidence to request only with permission

For one representative engagement, request owner-approved examples of:

- initial inquiry or introduction;
- discovery notes / FGD structure;
- proposal or scope;
- program design or run-of-show;
- calendar/logistics artifacts;
- facilitator notes template;
- client debrief;
- pre/post or follow-up measurement;
- invoice/payment-status reference;
- follow-up or renewal record.

Do not upload private client or participant content into public GitHub or semantic memory. Redact or use structure-only examples when possible.

## Immediate outputs from the conversation

The interview is complete only when it produces:

1. owner-approved vocabulary map;
2. one end-to-end real workflow map;
3. Cherry decision-authority matrix;
4. role map;
5. systems-of-record inventory;
6. data-sensitivity map;
7. first production workflow selection;
8. explicit list of owner dislikes / anti-patterns the product must avoid.

## Default first live workflow recommendation

Unless Cherry's answers point elsewhere, begin with:

**Secure Discovery Intake → Human Review → Cherry Judgment Queue**

This is a recommendation, not a decision already made. It should be accepted or replaced by the owner based on actual workflow truth.
