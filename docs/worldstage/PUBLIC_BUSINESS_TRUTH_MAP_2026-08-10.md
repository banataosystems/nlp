## Evidence boundary

This document contains public-source facts and clearly labeled hypotheses only. It must not be treated as proof of WorldStage's private internal workflow, systems of record, commercial rules, participant-data handling, or Cherry's decision authority.

WorldStage publicly positions itself around workplace transformation through people, talent and culture rather than generic training delivery. Current public language repeatedly emphasizes:

- current-state understanding before intervention;

- Team Building & Culture Development;
- Learning & Development;
- Keynotes & Motivation;
- safe learning environments;
- sustainable change and post-intervention follow-through;
- evidence/analytics after intervention;
- head, heart and gut engagement;

These terms should be preserved as candidate canonical vocabulary until Cherry/WorldStage validates or corrects them.

## Public service structure

### Team Building & Culture Development

WorldStage describes customized team/culture interventions spanning bonding, strengthening, awakening and broader culture-building needs. Public pages explicitly reject cookie-cutter teambuilding and ask about team theme, focus/pain points and attendee context before design.

### Learning & Development

Public material describes Fire University as a learning/development platform with leadership, communications, negotiations, selling, presentation and data-storytelling themes. An older public page also describes corporate-academy design from concept to execution and a learning-management/rewards layer.

### Keynotes & Motivation

WorldStage publicly markets keynote rallies and motivation, including Cherry Africa and other speakers, with emphasis on energy, mindset, emotional state and action.

## Public operating rhythm

The strongest current public process evidence is:

1. **Pre-training alignment** — FGDs, goal-setting meetings and discovery sessions to understand future ambition and current state.
2. **Intense intervention / boot camp** — inspiration and skills intended to bridge current state to ambition.
3. **Post-training / intervention alignment** — analytics from session data plus recommended next steps intended to sustain change.

This public process strongly supports the product model:

`Current reality → Discovery/alignment → Intervention design/delivery → Evidence/analytics → Sustainment`

It does **not** prove the exact internal sales, proposal, scheduling, staffing, invoicing or client-approval workflow.

## Systems-of-record hypotheses — NOT FACTS

Public evidence only allows these **hypotheses to validate**, not implementation assumptions:

1. website and event-site forms may create inquiry/registration records;
2. email may be an important client handoff channel;
3. event ticket/order surfaces may maintain event registration/cart data;
4. WorldStage may use separate internal tools for proposals, calendars, participant lists, analytics and finance;
5. Fire University may have or require a learning-management layer.

None of these hypotheses should drive production integration until Cherry/team identifies the actual authoritative systems.

## Product implications supported by public evidence

Safe conclusions for product design:

- keep Discovery before solution recommendation;
- model current state and desired state explicitly;
- allow FGD/discovery evidence to remain source-linked;
- separate intervention design from generic catalog selection;
- support team-building, L&D and keynote/motivation engagement families;
- preserve FIRE/WorldStage vocabulary rather than generic CRM language;
- maintain post-intervention evidence/analytics and sustainment actions;
- support event/public-experience workflows as a separate module rather than forcing them into client-transformation records;
- keep all public-source claims traceable and distinguish current from legacy pages.
