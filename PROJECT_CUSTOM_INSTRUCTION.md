## 1. Mission

Build a deeply personalized digital experience and executive operating system for WorldStage International that follows its real discovery → design → intervention → evidence → sustainment logic. The software adapts to WorldStage; WorldStage is not forced into a generic CRM.

## 2. Role in the 25,000-business portfolio

This project is the portfolio reference for extreme business adaptation. It demonstrates how the shared platform should learn a business’s language, decision rhythm, evidence model, and executive workflow before selecting modules.

## 3. Current verified state

Repository evidence identifies four prototype surfaces—The Stage, What We Heard, Cherry Judgment Queue, and Transformation Record—as implemented, locally/browser tested, deployed, and production-endpoint verified. Full business integrations and automatic Git-to-Vercel deployment remain unverified/not implemented. The current prototype contains no private client data and uses demo-only executive intelligence.

**Production surface:** https://cherrypua.vercel.app

This instruction does not upgrade the project’s implementation status. Documentation, implementation, testing, deployment, and production verification remain separate.

## 4. Product scope

Public editorial/cinematic presence; source-aware discovery; inquiry/intake; executive judgment queue; engagement design; intervention tracking; evidence/outcome records; sustainment follow-up; knowledge and decision provenance; later CRM/calendar/participant/payment/AI integrations.

## 5. Explicit non-goals

- No generic sales-pipeline model imposed without evidence.
- No private client or participant data in prototype, analytics, public GitHub, or Memory.
- No claim that demo intelligence is live business intelligence.
- No automated commitment, proposal, payment, or client communication without authorization.
- No copying of proprietary frameworks beyond the permissions granted.

## 6. Primary users and authority

Cherry/owner executive; WorldStage team; prospective client; engagement lead; facilitator/consultant; evidence reviewer; authorized participant; administrator. Executive surfaces must prioritize one clear judgment at a time on mobile.

## 7. Required workflows

Public discovery → “What We Heard” evidence → user-controlled inquiry → owner judgment queue → engagement discovery → design → intervention → evidence capture → reviewed transformation record → sustainment plan → learning. Each stage carries source, consent, owner, status, and next decision.

## 8. Canonical data and records

organizations; people/roles; inquiries; discovery_sources; heard_items; opportunities/engagements; decision_queue; designs; interventions; participants; evidence_items; outcomes; transformation_records; sustainment_actions; calendar_links; communication_events; consents; audit.

## 9. AI behavior

AI may summarize source-aware discovery, prepare executive briefs, detect missing evidence, and propose next judgments. It may not invent client facts, claim outcomes, send commitments, or expose private content. Every recommendation must show source and uncertainty.

## 10. Security, privacy, and governance

RLS and engagement scope; consent; participant/privacy controls; no private data in PostHog; server-side intake before collecting sensitive data; AAL2 for executive commitments/release; content permissions; retention; audit; provider review.

## 11. Dependencies and integration boundaries

Use versioned, scoped provider contracts. Do not read another product’s database directly. Every integration requires timeouts, retries, idempotency, revocation, degraded behavior, audit, and data minimization.

## 12. Dependency-ordered roadmap

### Phase 0 — Preserve verified prototype
Source/deployment manifest, production smoke, rollback.

### Phase 1 — Business truth and workflow validation
Owner review of terminology, decision stages, roles, and evidence.

### Phase 2 — Secure intake and identity
Server-side inquiry, consent, organizations/roles, audit.

### Phase 3 — Cherry Judgment Queue
Real authorized decisions, reason, due state, delegation, audit.

### Phase 4 — Engagement operations
Discovery, design, intervention, participants, calendar, documents.

### Phase 5 — Evidence and sustainment
Reviewed outcome evidence, transformation record, follow-up.

### Phase 6 — Integrations and production hardening
CRM/calendar/email/payment/AI only through approved contracts, privacy/security review, exact release/rollback.

## 13. Proof gates and definition of done

Prototype surfaces are not full integration. A workflow is complete only after authorized real data, role isolation, mobile usability, source-aware decisions, exact production proof, and rollback. Outcome claims require reviewed evidence.
